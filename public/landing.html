Safe by design

A live app is only fun when people feel safe. Here is what we do about it.

  • Verified hosts. Every host passes an ID check before going live.
  • Moderation around the clock. AI checks chat and reports as they come in, and a person decides on anything serious.
  • You're in control. Block anyone, report a stream or message in two taps, and room admins can mute or remove people.
  • Secure payments. Coins are bought through Stripe; your card details never touch our servers.

Questions

Is Zynalive free?

Yes. Watching lives, chatting and joining party rooms is free. Coins for gifts are optional.

Who can go live?

Anyone 18 or older who verifies their identity as a host. Viewers can join without verifying.

Where can I use it?

In any modern browser at zynalive.com, and in the Zynalive Android app (download the APK).

How do I delete my account?

In the app go to Me → Settings → Delete account, or follow the steps on our account deletion page.
