For agencies

Build a team of live hosts

Agencies are Zynalive's partners on the ground. You find talented hosts, help them start well and grow with them, with your own agency code and portal.

Apply to become an agency How hosting works

Already an agency? Open your portal at agency.zynalive.com.

What you get

Your agency code

Hosts enter your code when they apply, so they join your team automatically once they are verified.

Agency portal

See all your hosts at agency.zynalive.com or in the app: how many are verified, who is live right now and whose application is in review.

Shared success

Agencies earn when their community grows. Commission terms are agreed with Zynalive when your agency joins.

How it works

From application to your first live host, usually within a week.

  1. ApplyEmail support@zynalive.com with your agency name, city, phone number and how many hosts you work with.
  2. Meet usWe talk through your plans, the rules for hosts and the commission terms.
  3. Get your codeYour agency is set up in Zynalive and you receive your agency code and portal access.
  4. Bring your hostsHosts apply in the app with your code and verify their identity. You follow them in the portal.

What we expect

Agencies represent Zynalive, so we hold them to a high standard.

  • Adults only. Every host is 18 or older and passes an ID check. Never recruit anyone younger.
  • Honest recruiting. No fees from hosts to join, no false promises about earnings.
  • Safe content. Help your hosts follow the community rules; repeated breaks can end an agency partnership.
  • Real people. No fake accounts, bought gifts or gift farming. We check payments and gifts for fraud.
