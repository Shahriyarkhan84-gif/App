Ways to go live

Pick the format that suits you, or mix them through the week.

Solo live

Your camera, your chat. Pick a title, a category and a cover photo and you are live in seconds.

Party rooms

Host a voice or video room with seats. Invite guests up, run games and keep the room busy.

PK battles

Challenge another host. Both audiences cheer with gifts until the timer runs out; the bigger score wins.

Events and rankings

Climb the weekly rankings and join events to get featured in front of more viewers.

How to become a host

Every host joins through an agency, which helps you get started and grow. Ask your agency for its code, or find out about agencies.

  1. Create your free accountSign up with email, Google or Apple in the app or at zynalive.com.
  2. Apply to hostIn the app open Me, tap Agency → Join, enter your agency code and fill in the short form.
  3. Verify your identityScan your CNIC or passport and take a selfie. It takes a few minutes and keeps Zynalive safe.
  4. Go live and get paidTrack gifts on your host dashboard and earnings screen, then request a withdrawal.

Questions

How much do hosts earn?

Viewers buy coins and send them as gifts. You keep 90% of the coins from every gift you receive; the rest supports the stream and the platform. Your earnings screen shows your balance and what it is worth when you withdraw.

How do I get paid?

Request a withdrawal from the earnings screen to Easypaisa, JazzCash or a Pakistani bank account. Each withdrawal is checked by our team before it is paid.

Why do I need an agency code?

Agencies recruit and support hosts, and they help us keep hosting safe. If you don't have one yet, contact us and we will put you in touch with an agency.

What can't I do on a live?

No nudity, hate, violence, scams, or anyone under 18 on camera. Read the safety centre and our terms before you go live.
