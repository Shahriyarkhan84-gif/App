About us

Live, made for Pakistan

Zynalive is where people meet live: to sing, talk, play and cheer each other on. We are building it for Pakistan first, in the languages people speak and with the payment methods they use.

What we believe

  • Hosts should earn fairly. Hosts get 90% of every gift as beans and withdraw to Easypaisa, JazzCash or their bank.
  • Safety comes first. Verified adult hosts, moderation around the clock and simple tools to block and report.
  • Your language, your phone. English, Urdu, Hindi and Bengali, and an app that runs well on everyday Android phones and in the browser.
  • No ads. We earn from optional diamonds, not from selling your attention or your data.
